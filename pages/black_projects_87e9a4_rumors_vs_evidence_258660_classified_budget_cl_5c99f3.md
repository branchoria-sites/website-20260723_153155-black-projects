## Introduction

A classified budget line is not, by itself, evidence that a secret aircraft exists. Governments routinely classify funding for intelligence, electronic warfare, cyber capabilities, space systems, sensors and other sensitive activities that have nothing to do with new aircraft. The evidential value of a classified appropriation emerges only when it fits a broader pattern: the money appears in the right organisation, at the right time, in the right scale, and alongside independent signs such as facility expansion, contractor activity, flight testing or later [declassified records]({{ 'declassified/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12943" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: congress.gov">[Congress.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">congress.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 14, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 14, 2025</span></span></span>

This distinction is crucial when evaluating rumours about [black projects]({{ 'black-projects/' | relative_url }}). History shows that genuine classified aircraft programmes left financial traces, but those traces became meaningful only after they could be connected to multiple independent forms of evidence. Conversely, many famous rumours have relied on isolated budget anomalies that never developed into a coherent documentary or physical record.

## What classified appropriations can and cannot prove

Public defence budgets sometimes identify funding only as "classified programs" or provide little more than a lump-sum appropriation. These entries demonstrate that Congress authorised money for sensitive activities, not what those activities were.

Modern Department of Defense budgets illustrate this limitation. The Congressional Research Service notes that billions of dollars are requested annually for classified programmes spread across research, procurement and intelligence accounts. These funds support a wide variety of missions, including intelligence collection, reconnaissance, space systems, command-and-control, electronic warfare and other capabilities. A classified line item therefore cannot be assumed to represent a secret aircraft simply because the underlying purpose is withheld.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12943" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: congress.gov">[Congress.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">congress.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 14, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 14, 2025</span></span></span>

Likewise, some classified spending is deliberately aggregated to protect operational details. Congress still appropriates the funds and designated committees receive additional classified information, but the public documentation intentionally lacks the specificity needed to identify an individual programme. The absence of detail is therefore a feature of the budgeting system rather than evidence of an extraordinary hidden technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12943" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: congress.gov">[Congress.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">congress.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 14, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 14, 2025</span></span></span>

## How timing, scale and institutional location add weight

Budget evidence becomes substantially more persuasive when three factors align.

**Timing.** A significant increase in classified research funding is more meaningful if it coincides with known infrastructure growth, specialised hiring, contractor expansion or reports of flight testing. If those developments occur years apart, the apparent connection weakens.

**Scale.** Developing a sophisticated military aircraft requires sustained investment over multiple budget cycles. A single unexplained increase is less informative than a consistent pattern of research, development, testing and procurement funding that matches the expected lifecycle of a major aerospace programme.

**Institutional location.** Where the money is appropriated matters as much as how much is spent. Classified funding directed to organisations with established responsibilities for advanced aerospace development—such as portions of the Department of the Air Force or intelligence-related defence organisations—is inherently more relevant to aircraft claims than classified funding within unrelated agencies. The Congressional Research Service notes that much classified Department of Defense funding resides within Air Force-managed accounts as well as intelligence organisations such as the National Reconnaissance Office, reflecting their central roles in sensitive aerospace and intelligence programmes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12943" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: congress.gov">[Congress.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">congress.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 14, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 14, 2025</span></span></span>

None of these indicators is decisive alone. Their value lies in convergence with independent evidence rather than in the budget documents themselves.

## Why confirmed programmes look different from rumours

Historical black aircraft programmes demonstrate that budget clues eventually became understandable because other evidence accumulated around them.

After declassification, programmes such as the U-2, A-12 OXCART and [F-117]({{ 'f-117/' | relative_url }}) could be connected to [contractors]({{ 'contractors/' | relative_url }}), test facilities, procurement decisions, operational units and dated development milestones. Budgetary secrecy formed one strand within a much larger evidential network rather than serving as the primary proof.

By contrast, the long-running "Aurora" hypersonic aircraft rumour illustrates the opposite pattern. During the late 1980s and early 1990s, observers pointed to unexplained budget entries, particularly references interpreted as "Aurora," as supposed confirmation of a secret aircraft. Subsequent investigations found that the budget references were insufficient to establish that such an aircraft programme existed. Without corroborating documentation, sustained contractor evidence or official records emerging over time, the financial anomaly remained exactly that: an anomaly rather than confirmation.

The lesson is methodological rather than historical. Real aircraft programmes tend to accumulate reinforcing evidence across independent domains, whereas rumours often depend on repeatedly reinterpreting the same ambiguous budget reference.

## Common traps in code names, transfers and accounting gaps

Budget analysis becomes unreliable when ordinary defence accounting practices are mistaken for evidence of hidden aircraft.

Several recurring pitfalls deserve particular caution:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Assuming every code name identifies one aircraft.** Budget identifiers often represent broad portfolios, enabling technologies or administrative funding mechanisms rather than a single platform.
* **Confusing transfers with new programmes.** Funds may move between organisations for management or oversight reasons without reflecting the creation of a new capability.
* **Ignoring multi-purpose appropriations.** One classified research account may finance numerous unrelated projects simultaneously, making it impossible to infer a single outcome from the public budget description.
* **Treating missing information as positive evidence.** Classification creates information gaps intentionally. Those gaps increase uncertainty; they do not increase the probability that a specific rumour is correct.

</div>

Congress has also identified the complexity created by "pass-through" funding, in which money may be requested through one organisation while primarily supporting another. Such arrangements complicate public interpretation of budgets and illustrate why institutional context is essential before drawing conclusions about any particular aircraft claim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12943" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: congress.gov">[Congress.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">congress.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 14, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 14, 2025</span></span></span>

## Budget clues are strongest when they converge with other evidence

The most reliable use of classified budget information is as one component within a broader evidential framework.

A classified appropriation deserves increased attention when it aligns with multiple independent indicators such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* expansion of secure aerospace facilities;
* procurement records pointing to specialised infrastructure;
* contractor hiring for matching technical skills;
* documented flight-test activity in the appropriate period;
* later declassified programme histories that retrospectively explain the spending.

</div>

Conversely, a classified budget line that stands alone, without matching institutional, technical or chronological evidence, remains weak support for claims about a secret aircraft.

Government oversight bodies reinforce this distinction. Reviews by the Government [Accountability]({{ 'accountability/' | relative_url }}) Office acknowledge that highly classified Special Access Programs exist and receive dedicated oversight, while also showing that these programmes remain subject to budgeting, auditing and congressional review within secure channels.     var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
