## Introduction

One of the most important reasons that UFO reports surged during the Cold War was not extraterrestrial activity but the arrival of aircraft that were operating far beyond what the public believed was technologically possible. The Lockheed [U-2]({{ 'u-2/' | relative_url }}) spy plane, first tested in deep secrecy during the mid-1950s, could fly above 60,000 feet at a time when commercial airliners typically cruised between 10,000 and 20,000 feet and many military aircraft remained below 40,000 feet. ## What made U-2 flights look unusual

The U-2 entered service at a moment when public expectations about aviation lagged far behind classified technology. To most people in the 1950s, an aircraft operating at more than 60,000 feet was almost unimaginable. Even experienced airline crews rarely encountered aircraft at such heights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/disasters-and-phenomena/u2s-ufos-and-operation-blue-book.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">u2s ufos and operation blue book</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandU-2s, UFOs, and Operation Blue Book24 Jan 2024 — High-altitude testing of the U-2 soon led to an unexpe...</span></span></span>

Several characteristics made the aircraft especially likely to generate UFO reports:

* **Extreme altitude:** Observers could see an object far above normal air traffic, apparently isolated in a part of the sky where no aircraft were expected.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/disasters-and-phenomena/u2s-ufos-and-operation-blue-book.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">u2s ufos and operation blue book</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandU-2s, UFOs, and Operation Blue Book24 Jan 2024 — High-altitude testing of the U-2 soon led to an unexpe...</span></span></span>
* **Sunlight effects:** Early U-2 aircraft had reflective surfaces that could catch sunlight long after lower-altitude aircraft had entered darkness. To people on the ground, these aircraft could appear as glowing or fiery objects moving through an otherwise dark sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sgp.fas.org/library/ciaufo.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sgp.fas.org">[FAS Project on Government Secrecy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sgp.fas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FAS Project on Government SecrecyCIA&#x27;s Role in the Study of UFOs, 1947-90Air Force BLUE BOOK investigators aware of the secret U-2 flight...</span></span></span>

## Why investigators could not explain everything

The existence of the U-2 created a dilemma for American authorities. Air Force investigators working on Project [Blue Book]({{ 'blue-book/' | relative_url }}) were tasked with evaluating UFO reports, but many sightings involved aircraft whose existence was itself classified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The project closed in 1969 and we have no...Read more...</span></span></span>

According to declassified accounts, Blue Book personnel regularly compared UFO reports with U-2 flight records. In many cases they could determine that a reported object corresponded to a known reconnaissance flight. However, investigators could not simply tell witnesses that they had observed a secret spy aircraft. Doing so would have revealed a programme considered critical to national security.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/research/library/online-reading-room/title-list-alphabetically/u/u2s-ufos-and-operation-blue-book.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval History and Heritage Command U-2s, UFOs, and Operation Blue Book</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">reports of unidentified flying objects (UFOs). ## Why the U-2 remains a key lesson in black-project mythology

The U-2 story demonstrates how advanced technology can appear supernatural when it is observed outside the limits of public knowledge. Witnesses were often reporting something real: a genuine aircraft operating in the sky.
